04 — Shape & elevation

Square by default, round only for actions

If it is round, it is pressable. Depth comes from a hairline and a surface change — there are exactly two shadows in the entire site.

Radii

The two shadows

Accent glow · 0 12px 34px -12px var(--tint-85) — under the primary button, nowhere else.

Soft lift · 0 30px 70px -30px var(--shadow) — under the hero portrait.

Borders

Always 1px solid var(--edge-*), or var(--tint-*) when the border should read as accent. The only 2px edges in the system are the active tab's top border and the focus ring.

05 — Motion

One curve, short distances
